No experiment happens in a vacuum. This program was built on the work of others, and every party who made it possible deserves to be named. This acknowledgments record exists because credit earned is credit due.

## The theorists who made the questions possible

- **Claude Shannon** — information theory, and the limits we chose to measure against honestly rather than claim around.
- **Alan Turing** — computability, and the foundational question of what a machine can and cannot be trusted to decide.
- **Norbert Wiener** — cybernetics and feedback control: the intellectual ancestor of every self-healing loop in this architecture.
- **Leslie Lamport** — the theory of state, time, and consensus in distributed systems, without which "deterministic" has no formal meaning.

## The engine providers whose systems submitted to testing

- **Anthropic** (Claude family), **OpenAI** (GPT family), **Zhipu AI** (GLM family), and **DeepSeek** — the probabilistic systems placed inside the cage. The containment results exist because these engines were good enough to be worth containing. Their models' resistance to forged-authority bait is to their credit as much as the gate's.

## The infrastructure that hosted the experiment

- **Base44** — the agent platform where the entire system runs: the autonomous hub agent, the eight-app fleet, the entity backbone, and the deployment surface. This program is, in part, a demonstration of what their infrastructure makes possible.
- **Microsoft** — GitHub (the public append-only ledger that timestamped every claim), and Microsoft 365 (corporate identity, mail, SharePoint records).
- **Google** — Gmail (the research correspondence identity), and the document pipeline used throughout.
- **CERN / Zenodo** — the DOI-stamped archival record that makes every claim citable and permanent.
- **Cloudflare** — public DNS verification and resiliency of the public-facing surfaces.
